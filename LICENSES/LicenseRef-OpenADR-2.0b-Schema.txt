OpenADR 2.0b XML Schema files

These XML Schema (XSD) files are part of the OpenADR 2.0b schema distributed by
the OpenADR Alliance (https://www.openadr.org). Several of them are profiles of
schemas published by OASIS (Energy Interoperation, EMIX), the IETF (xCal,
Atom), the W3C (XML, XML Signature) and NAESB (ESPI / Green Button).

The files themselves do not contain an explicit license statement. They are
redistributed unmodified, in good faith, because they are required to validate
OpenADR 2.0b messages. They must not be modified.

If you hold rights in these files and believe the attribution or licensing
information is incorrect or incomplete, please file an issue at
https://github.com/OpenLEADR/openleadr-python/issues.
